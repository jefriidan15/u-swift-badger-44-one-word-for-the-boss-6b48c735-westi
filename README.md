## The word

> hovercraft

He hovers. Over your shoulder, over the deadline, over the meeting that could have been an email — always hovering, never landing, and somehow always there the second you lean back.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the word — exactly one word, 10 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`. No randomness, no input: the output is byte-identical on every run.

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
